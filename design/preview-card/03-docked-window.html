Chromium (web browser)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Chromium is a free and open-source web browser project, mainly developed and maintained by Google. It is the base for browsers such as Brave, Microsoft Edge and Opera.

Agent control

The browser is driven through the Chrome DevTools Protocol: the agent clicks, types and scrolls inside the page, while the pointer you are using stays exactly where it was.

Every open tab is a target on that endpoint, which is what the panel on the right reads every three seconds. Picking one activates it through the same endpoint.

Targets

The panel asks http://127.0.0.1:64659/json/list for the target list and shows one row per page: its index, its title and its URL. A row you pick is activated with one more call to the same endpoint.

Nothing leaves the machine, and the mod never reads a password, a cookie or a profile.
